### V2.10C — Missed Opportunity Analysis ✅

- **`MissedOpportunityAnalyzer`** with `analyze_misses()`:

  - Miss reasons: missing engine data, ineligibility, selection constraints
  - Preserves structured intelligence from `OpportunityResult`
- **Tests**: `test_missed_opportunity_analyzer_identifies_high_score_not_selected`, `test_missed_opportunity_does_not_flag_low_score`, `test_missed_opportunity_uses_missing_engines_reason`, `test_missed_opportunity_records_correct_fields`, `test_missed_opportunity_zero_execution_coupling` — all PASSED (5/5)
- **AST verification**: Zero forbidden imports (paper_trading, broker, execution, order, position, signal)

### V2.10D — Replay Validation / Calibration Foundation ✅
- **Migration**: `20261109190000_v29e_replay_validation.py` — creates `replay_calibration` table with:
  - `calibrated_score`, `calibration_factor`, `missing_engine_adjustment`
  - `baseline_score`, `calibration_type`, `engine_coverage_pct`
  - `observation_count_at_ts`, `calibrated_at`, `validation_status`
  - Unique constraint on `(canonical_contract_id, observed_at, calibration_type)`
- **`ReplayValidator`** class:
  - `validate_observation_at()` — point-in-time observation validation with coverage percentage
  - `calibrate_score()` — score adjustment based on engine coverage (80%+ → no adjustment, 50-80% → -10%, 20-50% → -25%, <20% → -50%)
  - `validate_replay_fidelity()` — no look-ahead leakage verification
  - `_record_calibration()` — audit trail stub for pipeline tuning
- **Tests**: `test_replay_validator_validates_observation_exists`, `test_replay_validator_missing_observation`, `test_replay_validator_calibrate_score_full_coverage`, `test_replay_validator_calibrate_score_partial_coverage`, `test_replay_validator_calibrate_score_zero_coverage`, `test_replay_validator_validate_replay_fidelity`, `test_replay_validator_validate_replay_fidelity_missing`, `test_replay_validator_zero_execution_coupling`, `test_calibration_record_preserves_point_in_time` — all PASSED (9/9)
